---
title: Why do software systems get harder to change over time?
summary: Long-lived systems seem to accumulate complexity and resist modification. Is this an inevitable law of evolving software, or a contingent outcome?
area: evolution
---

## Why it matters

Most software cost is incurred after first release. If decay is inevitable, maintenance budgets should plan for it; if it is contingent, its causes can be identified and managed.

## Scope

Includes the growth, complexity, and changeability of systems across releases, in both industrial and open source contexts.
