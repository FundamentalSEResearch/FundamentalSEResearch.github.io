---
title: Does geographic distance between developers harm software outcomes?
summary: Distributed development is now normal. Do work items and components built across sites take longer or end up with more defects than those built by co-located people?
area: teams
---

## Why it matters

Organizations choose between co-locating teams and distributing them for cost, talent, or time-zone reasons. If distance harms speed or quality, the savings must be weighed against it and work should be split to keep tightly coupled work together; if not, distribution is cheaper than it seems.

## Scope

Includes the effect of geographic and organizational distance on development speed and software quality. Excludes communication tools themselves, except as a moderating factor.
