---
title: Does involving more people in reviewing code make software more reliable?
summary: Open source rests partly on the idea that many reviewers catch more bugs. Do more eyes reduce defects, or do more hands on the same code introduce them?
area: quality
---

## Why it matters

Organizations decide how many reviewers to require, how widely to open their code, and how much review participation to expect. If more reviewers reliably reduce defects, these are cheap levers for quality; if involvement by many loosely connected developers adds defects, the lever cuts both ways.

## Scope

Includes code review coverage and participation, and the number and organization of developers who change or inspect a piece of code, related to defects and vulnerabilities. Excludes the effectiveness of specific review tools.
