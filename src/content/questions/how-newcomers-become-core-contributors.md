title: How do newcomers become core contributors in open source projects?
summary: Most people who show up in an open source project leave after a few contributions. What distinguishes the few who stay and move toward the core?
area: teams
---

## Why it matters

Projects depend on replacing core members who leave. Understanding how newcomers stay and grow tells projects whether to lower entry barriers, mentor, or create peripheral tasks, and tells researchers whether participation is driven by initial motivation or by what happens after joining.

## Scope

Includes the trajectories of newcomers from first contact to sustained or core participation, and the activities and social processes along the way. Excludes the barriers that prevent people from making a first contribution at all.
