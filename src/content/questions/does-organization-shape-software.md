## Why it matters

If system architecture tends to mirror organizational structure, then reorganizing teams is also an architectural decision, and architectural goals (such as modularity) can be pursued by designing the organization.

## Scope

Includes the correspondence between team or firm boundaries and module boundaries, and the effect of organizational structure on product quality. Excludes the reverse question of how architecture should drive team design, except where studies test it.
