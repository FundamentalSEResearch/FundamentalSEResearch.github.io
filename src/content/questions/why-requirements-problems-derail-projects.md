---
title: Are requirements problems the main cause of software project failure?
summary: Projects fail for many reasons, from technology to staffing. Do incomplete, ambiguous, or changing requirements account for more of the trouble than anything else?
area: requirements
---

## Why it matters

If requirements problems dominate, investment in elicitation, validation, and requirements management pays off more than investment elsewhere; if they are one cause among many, that investment should be balanced against others.

## Scope

Includes the frequency, causes, and effects of requirements problems such as incompleteness, ambiguity, and volatility, and their contribution to project problems and failure. Excludes the effectiveness of specific requirements notations.
