title: What motivates developers to contribute to open source software?
summary: Many developers contribute to open source for no pay, others are paid to. Is contribution driven by enjoyment, by career and status, or by need, and do payments undermine voluntary motivation?
area: people
---

## Why it matters

Companies increasingly pay people to work on open source. If extrinsic rewards crowd out the intrinsic motivation that sustains communities, paid participation could weaken them; if not, payment is a safe way to strengthen them.

## Scope

Includes intrinsic and extrinsic motivations of open source developers and their relation to participation and performance. Excludes motivation in purely commercial teams.
