---
title: Is systematic test selection more effective than random testing?
summary: Partition testing divides the input space and tests each part; random testing samples inputs without a plan. Does the extra effort of systematic selection find more faults?
area: quality
---

## Why it matters

Systematic test design is labour-intensive, while random testing is cheap and easy to automate. If random testing performs nearly as well, effort can shift to running more tests; if systematic selection wins, the design effort is justified.

## Scope

Includes the fault-detecting ability of partition-based and random test selection, studied analytically, by simulation, or empirically. Excludes specific test generation tools.
