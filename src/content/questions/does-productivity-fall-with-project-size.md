---
title: Does productivity fall as software projects grow larger?
summary: Estimation models often assume that doubling a system's size more than doubles its cost. Are there diseconomies of scale in software development, or economies, or neither?
area: planning
---

## Why it matters

Whether productivity falls with size determines how estimates should scale, whether large projects should be split, and how productivity can be compared across projects of different sizes.

## Scope

Includes the relationship between project or product size and effort or productivity across sets of projects. Excludes the effect of adding people to a single late project, covered by a separate question.
