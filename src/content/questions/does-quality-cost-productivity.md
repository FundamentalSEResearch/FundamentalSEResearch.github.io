---
title: Does investing in quality slow development down?
summary: A common belief is that quality and speed must be traded off. An older claim says the opposite, that higher quality pays for itself by reducing rework. Which holds?
area: planning
---

## Why it matters

Deadlines push teams to cut quality activities. If quality work reduces total effort and cycle time, cutting it is a false economy; if it truly costs time, the trade-off must be managed explicitly.

## Scope

Includes the relationship between product quality, or the effort spent achieving it, and development effort and cycle time. Excludes the cost of failures after delivery.
