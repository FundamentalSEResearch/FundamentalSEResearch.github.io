---
title: Why are software effort estimates systematically too optimistic?
summary: Overruns are the norm rather than the exception. Is this a property of people, of the information they are given, or of the tasks themselves?
area: planning
---

## Why it matters

Estimates drive budgets, staffing, and commitments. If the error has a predictable direction and cause, it can be corrected; if it is noise, the response should be different (buffers, iterative re-planning).

## Scope

Includes judgment-based (expert) estimation of effort and duration, and the cognitive and contextual factors that bias it. The question itself is open to the possibility that estimates are *not* uniformly optimistic.
