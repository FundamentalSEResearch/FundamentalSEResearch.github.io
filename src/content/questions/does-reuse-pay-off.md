---
title: Does reusing software improve productivity and quality?
summary: Building software from existing components has been promoted since the 1960s. Do projects that reuse more deliver faster and with fewer defects?
area: design
---

## Why it matters

Reuse programs, component libraries, and open source dependencies all assume that reuse pays. If benefits are real and consistent, reuse deserves investment; if they depend on conditions, those conditions need to be known.

## Scope

Includes systematic and ad hoc reuse of code and components, related to productivity, defect density, and time to market. Excludes the security risks of third-party dependencies.
