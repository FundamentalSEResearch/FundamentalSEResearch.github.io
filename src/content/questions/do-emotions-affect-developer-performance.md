---
title: Do developers' emotions affect how well they work?
summary: Programming is cognitively demanding work. Do developers who feel better solve problems better, or is mood irrelevant to performance?
area: people
---

## Why it matters

If affect influences performance, working conditions, team climate, and management practices that affect mood are levers for productivity and quality, not only for well-being.

## Scope

Includes developers' affective states, such as happiness, frustration, or anxiety, related to problem-solving performance, productivity, and quality. Excludes long-term job satisfaction and turnover intention.
