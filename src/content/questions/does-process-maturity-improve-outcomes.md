---
title: Does a more mature development process produce better software?
summary: Maturity models claim that defined, measured, and continuously improved processes lead to better results. Do organizations that move up the maturity scale actually deliver higher quality and productivity?
area: design
---

## Why it matters

Organizations spend heavily on process improvement and assessments. If maturity improves outcomes, that spending is justified; if the effects are small or confined to certain contexts, the money may be better spent elsewhere.

## Scope

Includes process maturity as assessed by models such as the CMM, related to quality, cycle time, and effort. Excludes comparisons between named development methods.
