---
title: Does AI assistance make developers more productive?
summary: Tools that generate code from natural language are spreading rapidly. Do they help developers finish real work faster, and does the answer depend on the developer and the task?
area: people
---

## Why it matters

Organizations are making large investments and staffing decisions based on expected productivity gains from AI assistance. If gains depend strongly on task and expertise, blanket expectations will be wrong in both directions.

## Scope

Includes the effect of AI code generation and assistance on the time and effort developers need to complete programming tasks, measured in experiments or field studies. Excludes the quality and security of generated code.
