---
title: How is work distributed among contributors in open source projects?
summary: Open source projects can attract thousands of contributors. Is development spread across them, or done by a small core with a large periphery around it?
area: teams
---

## Why it matters

If most work rests on a small core, a project's health depends on retaining a few people, and the coordination advantages of a small team may explain how open source scales. If work is spread more widely, other coordination mechanisms must be at work.

## Scope

Includes the share of code, fixes, and other contributions made by the most active developers, and how this varies across projects. Excludes the motives for contributing.
