title: How do team size and coordination affect software delivery speed?
summary: Adding people adds capacity, but also adds communication paths and dependencies. When does the coordination cost outweigh the extra hands?
area: teams
---

## Why it matters

Staffing is the most common lever managers pull when a project falls behind. If coordination overhead grows faster than capacity, that lever can backfire, and the decision of how to split work across people, teams, and sites becomes as important as how many people to hire.

## Scope

Includes team growth over time, distributed and multi-site development, and the alignment between who must coordinate and who actually does. Excludes individual developer productivity.
