---
title: Why do developers adopt or resist new tools and methods?
summary: Organizations regularly introduce new methodologies and tools, and developers regularly ignore them. Is adoption driven by perceived benefit, ease of use, social pressure, or mandate?
area: people
---

## Why it matters

A method or tool that is not used delivers none of its benefits. Knowing what drives developers' intentions to adopt tells organizations whether to invest in demonstrating benefits, simplifying the practice, building social support, or mandating its use.

## Scope

Includes individual developers' intentions to use and actual use of development methodologies and tools. Excludes the question of whether a given method is effective once adopted.
