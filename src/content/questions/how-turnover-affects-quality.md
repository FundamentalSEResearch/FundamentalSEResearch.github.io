title: How does developer turnover affect software quality?
summary: Developers join and leave projects all the time, taking knowledge with them. Do code bases touched by departing or newly arrived developers end up with more defects?
area: teams
---

## Why it matters

Turnover is constant in both companies and open source. If it measurably harms quality, retention, knowledge transfer, and onboarding are quality investments, and turnover can serve as a risk signal for testing and review.

## Scope

Includes developers leaving and joining organizations or projects, related to defects in the code they worked on. Excludes the causes of turnover.
