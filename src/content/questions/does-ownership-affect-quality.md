---
title: Does clear code ownership lead to higher software quality?
summary: Some components are written mostly by one person; others are touched by many occasional contributors. Do components with a clear owner have fewer defects?
area: quality
---

## Why it matters

Organizations decide whether to assign owners to components or let anyone change anything. If diffuse ownership leads to defects, ownership metrics can guide reviews and testing, and team policies can be designed accordingly.

## Scope

Includes the share of changes made by a component's main contributor and the number of minor contributors, related to pre- and post-release defects. Excludes legal ownership and licensing.
