title: How do software teams actually design and build software?
summary: Methods prescribe phases such as analysis, design, coding, and testing. Is that what developers actually do, or does development unfold some other way?
area: design
---

## Why it matters

Curricula, tools, and survey instruments are often built on the phases that methods prescribe. If real development does not follow those phases, research framed around them can misread what developers do, and practices built on them may fight the way teams actually work.

## Scope

Includes how individuals and teams move between understanding the problem, designing, and building, as observed in practice. Excludes the question of which method is most effective, which is a question about prescriptions rather than about what happens.
