## Why it matters

Inspections cost the time of several people. Knowing whether they pay off, and whether the expensive meeting adds defects beyond individual preparation, determines how review processes should be designed.

## Scope

Includes formal design and code inspections, their effect on quality and productivity, and the contribution of process elements such as meetings and team size. Excludes lightweight, tool-based code review, covered by a separate question.
