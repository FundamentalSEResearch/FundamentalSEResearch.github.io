---
title: Are defects concentrated in a small part of a software system?
summary: If most faults sit in a few modules, testing and review effort can be targeted. Is fault concentration a stable regularity, and do the same modules stay fault-prone over time?
area: quality
---

## Why it matters

Quality assurance budgets are finite. If faults follow a stable, skewed distribution, effort can be concentrated where faults are likely; if the distribution shifts between phases or releases, targeting based on past faults may miss where the next ones appear.

## Scope

Includes the distribution of faults across modules or files, whether fault-proneness persists across test phases and releases, and whether simple measures such as size account for it. Excludes the question of which prediction technique is most accurate.
