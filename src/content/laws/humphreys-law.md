## Statement

Defined, measured, and continuously improved processes make development more predictable and less error-prone, and so more productive.

## Notes on evidence

Studies of organizations that moved up the CMM levels report better quality and productivity. The direct effect of maturity on effort can be negative, since mature processes add work, with the net gain coming through improved quality.
