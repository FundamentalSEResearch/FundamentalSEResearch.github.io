## Statement

When predicting completion times, people take an *inside view*: they focus on the specific plan for the task at hand and neglect the distribution of outcomes for similar past tasks. The result is systematic underestimation.

## Notes on evidence

The effect is well documented in psychology. Reviews that include software tasks show the bias depends on task size and on how the estimate is requested, so "estimates are always too low" is too strong.
