## Statement

Building software from existing, proven components is cheaper and faster than building it from scratch, and the components are more reliable because they have already been used and fixed.

## Notes on evidence

Industrial studies agree that reused components have fewer defects. Evidence on productivity and time to market is positive but weaker, because measures differ and few studies control for confounding factors.
