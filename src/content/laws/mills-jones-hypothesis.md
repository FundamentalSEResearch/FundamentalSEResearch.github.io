## Statement

Quality and productivity are not opposites. Rework caused by defects dominates development cost, so efforts that reduce defects reduce cost and time overall.

## Notes on evidence

Empirical support comes mainly from studies of process improvement, which find that quality gains are accompanied by shorter cycle times and less effort, outweighing the direct cost of the quality activities.
