## Statement

Of all the things that can go wrong in a software project, deficient requirements are the most common and the most damaging.

## Notes on evidence

Glass derived the law from case studies of runaway projects. Later practitioner surveys consistently rank incomplete, ambiguous, and changing requirements among the top problems and as frequent causes of project failure, though they rely on practitioners' perceptions rather than measured outcomes.
