## Statement

Inspections catch errors before testing, when they are cheaper to fix, and so improve both quality and productivity. In Fagan's process the inspection meeting is the central activity.

## Notes on evidence

The overall benefit of inspections is well supported. The role of the meeting is not: experiments find that most defects are found during individual preparation and that meetings add few new ones relative to their cost.
