---
name: Simulation modeling
kind: research
summary: Build an executable model of a process (e.g., system dynamics) and explore how outcomes respond to changes in policy or parameters.
references: [kellner-madachy-raffo-1999]
---

Useful for exploring the dynamics of a theory and for "what-if" analysis. Results are only as valid as the model's structure and calibration, so they generate hypotheses rather than confirm them.
