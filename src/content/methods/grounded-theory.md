---
name: Grounded theory
kind: research
summary: Generate a theory from mostly qualitative data through iterative coding, constant comparison, and theoretical sampling, rather than testing a theory chosen in advance.
references: [stol-ralph-fitzgerald-2016]
---

A method for *building* theory, not testing it. Grounded theory is a family of methodologies (Glaserian, Straussian, and constructivist), and studies should say which one they follow. Its rigor comes from keeping the researcher close to the data, which guards against over-rationalizing what developers do.
