## Statement

Technical dependencies between pieces of work imply *coordination requirements* between the people doing that work. Congruence is the degree to which actual coordination (communication, shared team membership) covers those requirements. Higher congruence leads to faster task completion.

## Relationship to other theories

It operationalizes ideas from [Coordination Theory](/theories/coordination-theory/) and [Conway's Law](/theories/conways-law/) as a measurable quantity.
