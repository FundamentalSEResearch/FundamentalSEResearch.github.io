## Explanation

Negative emotions narrow attention toward specific actions, such as fleeing or attacking, which is useful in threatening situations. Positive emotions do the opposite: they widen attention and the set of ideas a person considers, which helps when a problem needs new approaches. Over time, the exploration they encourage builds skills and relationships that persist after the emotion has passed.

## Relevance to software engineering

Programming and design require analytic and creative problem solving. The theory predicts that developers in a more positive state solve such problems better, making affect a factor in performance rather than only in well-being.
