## Statement

Lehman's laws describe *E-type* systems, meaning software embedded in and used in the real world. The best known are:

- **Continuing change:** a system must be continually adapted, or it becomes progressively less satisfactory.
- **Increasing complexity:** as a system evolves, its complexity increases unless work is done to maintain or reduce it.
- **Continuing growth:** functional content must keep increasing to maintain user satisfaction.

Later formulations added further laws, such as declining growth rate and self-regulation.

## Notes on evidence

The evidence differs from law to law, so the laws should be evaluated individually rather than as a package.
