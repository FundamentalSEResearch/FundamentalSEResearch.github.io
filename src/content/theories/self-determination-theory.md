## Explanation

People are naturally inclined to engage in activities they find interesting, and this inclination flourishes when their needs for autonomy, competence, and relatedness are met. External rewards can either support motivation, when they are experienced as informative, or undermine it, when they are experienced as controlling and shift the perceived reason for acting from the self to the reward.

## Relevance to software engineering

Open source combines volunteers driven by enjoyment and learning with developers paid by firms. The theory predicts which motivations sustain contribution and warns that payment could crowd out the intrinsic motivation communities rely on.
