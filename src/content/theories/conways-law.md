## Statement

Any organization that designs a system will produce a design whose structure is a copy of the organization's communication structure.

## Mirroring hypothesis

In management research the same idea is studied as the *mirroring hypothesis*: the structure of a product's technical dependencies corresponds to the structure of the organization that develops it.

## Notes on evidence

Support is strong but not universal. Open collaborative settings appear to be a systematic exception.
